## First playable slice
One exploration-first course with a readable route to the summit, a rewarding detour that rejoins the route, and a way back after a fall. The originating A game stays available for comparison.

- The lead issued two independent builder briefs, covering gameplay and landscape.
- Gameplay acceptance requires an optional star, fall recovery without losing that star, a clear finish and clean restart. Keyboard and touch each support movement, jump and sprint, and the important cues work when muted.
- The scene should make the player, the next landing and the route fork recognizable. Before the first input, desktop shows the field under a shallow masthead; portrait shows a taller field with the movement pad opposite the action buttons. The HUD sits above both.
- Browser evidence covers small phone, tablet and desktop. The intended physical desktop frame budget is 16.7 ms; hardware measurement is pending the owner's walk.
- Current stage: gameplay and landscape builds, then independent play, visual and code reviews. Show the playable file and organized captures to the owner before final acceptance.
